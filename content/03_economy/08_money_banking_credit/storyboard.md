#### Right 40% Typography Content
- **Header**: MONEY CREATION
- **Subtitle**: Fractional Reserve Banking
- **Point 1**: **Fractional Reserves**: Banks hold a fraction of deposits and lend out the surplus.
- **Point 2**: **Bank Spread**: Profit earned between loan lending rates and deposit rates.
- **Point 3**: **Money Multiplier**: Credit expansion equals one divided by the Cash Reserve Ratio.
- **Takeaway Card**: *Higher reserve ratios contract total bank credit creation.*
