**Visual**: Seamless transition. Left 60%: Technical cutaway diagram displaying a horizontal price line intersected by a rising Marginal Cost curve. Right 40%: Formatted typography card.  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:  
"How does a firm decide its production volume? It compares market price with marginal cost. If price exceeds the cost of making one more unit, expand. If it falls below, cut back. Maximum profit occurs right where rising marginal cost matches market price."

"Watch out for this exam trap: a firm losing money does not shut down immediately! Fixed factory rent is already sunk. As long as market price covers daily variable costs, the firm keeps operating to pay down overhead. It shuts down only when price drops below Average Variable Cost."
