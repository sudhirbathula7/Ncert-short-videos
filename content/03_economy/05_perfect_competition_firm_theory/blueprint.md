# Topic 05: Architectural Blueprint & Pedagogical Framing

## 1. Syllabus & Exam Integration
- UPSC Prelims:
  - The 4 structural conditions of perfect competition.
  - Price-taking mechanics: Why the firm's demand line is perfectly horizontal (Price = AR = MR).
  - Identification of the Shut-Down Point (minimum AVC) versus the Break-Even Point (minimum SAC).
  - The derivation of individual supply from the rising section of Marginal Cost.
  - Long-run equilibrium condition: Free entry and exit driving economic profit to zero.
- UPSC Mains (GS Paper III - Indian Economy):
  - Farm gate dynamics: Smallholder agricultural markets behaving as price-takers with zero pricing leverage.
  - Production decisions during downturns: Industrial units continuing output as long as variable costs are covered.
  - Allocative efficiency: The contrast between competitive market efficiency and monopolistic exploitation.

## 2. Structural Breakdown (The 5 Core Learning Pillars)
1. Pillar 1: Anatomy of Perfect Competition — Atomistic structure, product identity, and price-taking.
2. Pillar 2: The Revenue Profile — Horizontal AR and MR lines and linear Total Revenue.
3. Pillar 3: Profit Maximisation Conditions — Equality of price and rising marginal cost.
4. Pillar 4: The Operational Buffer & Supply Curve — Shut-down versus break-even rules.
5. Pillar 5: Long-Run Free Entry Dynamics — Zero economic profit and market supply aggregation.

## 3. High-Yield Data & Benchmarks
- Individual Firm Demand: Infinitely elastic horizontal line.
- Short-Run Supply Curve: Rising SMC at and above the minimum point of AVC.
- Long-Run Supply Curve: Rising LRMC at and above the minimum point of LRAC.
- Shut-Down Threshold: Price falls below minimum AVC.
- Break-Even Threshold: Price equals minimum SAC.
