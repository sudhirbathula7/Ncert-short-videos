# Topic 13: Architectural Blueprint & Pedagogical Framing

## 1. Syllabus & Exam Integration
- **UPSC Prelims**:
  - Distinctions between World Bank World Development Report criteria (Gross National Income per capita) vs. UNDP Human Development Index (multidimensional health, schooling, income).
  - Measurement of Infant Mortality Rate (IMR), Literacy Rate definitions, Net Attendance Ratio (NAR).
  - Groundwater extraction trends and Central Ground Water Authority (CGWA) assessments.
- **UPSC Mains (GS Paper III - Indian Economy & Sustainable Development)**:
  - "Economic Growth vs. Economic Development": Why high GDP/PCI does not guarantee social development indicators.
  - Public goods failure and the imperative of collective social infrastructure (healthcare, education, sanitation).
  - Intergenerational equity and environmental sustainability in post-reform growth trajectories.

## 2. Structural Breakdown (The 5 Core Learning Pillars)
1. **Pillar 1: Relativity & Conflict of Development Goals** — Subjective aspirations and socio-ecological displacement.
2. **Pillar 2: Per Capita Income & The Inequality Mask** — Why statistical averages conceal wealth concentration.
3. **Pillar 3: The Punjab-Kerala Paradox** — Quantitative growth vs. qualitative social development indices.
4. **Pillar 4: UNDP's Human Development Framework** — The three-pronged composite indicator and South Asian comparisons.
5. **Pillar 5: Ecological Limits & Intergenerational Equity** — Groundwater depletion and resource thresholds.

## 3. High-Yield Data & Definitions
- **Infant Mortality Rate (IMR)**: Number of children dying before completing 1 year of age per 1,000 live births in that year.
- **Literacy Rate**: Proportion of literate population in the 7-and-above age bracket.
- **Net Attendance Ratio (NAR)**: Total number of children of age group 14–15 (or primary 6–10) attending school as a percentage of total children in that age group.
- **Groundwater Depletion Indicator**: ~33% of Indian blocks/districts currently overexploited or critical.
