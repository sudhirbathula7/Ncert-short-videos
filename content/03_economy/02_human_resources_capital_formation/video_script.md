### [0:00 - 0:14] Page 1: Population: Liability or Asset?
**Visual**: Clean off-white archival book page. Left side: classical etching of a crowded village square transforming through an architectural arch into a lecture hall of scholars and engineers. Right side empty warm ivory paper.  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:  
"Is a large population an economic curse or an asset? Sheer numbers mean nothing without human capital. Just as an economy turns raw iron into industrial machinery, it transforms people into human capital through education, healthcare, and skills. Population is only a resource when it is empowered."  
*(Right-Side Text Overlay: Animate yellow highlight over "People as Resource: Transforming Population into Productive Human Capital")*

### [0:14 - 0:28] Page 2: The 5 Engines of Human Capital
**Visual**: Page-turn curl sound effect. Left side: a compass rose linking five engraved symbols: a book (Education), a caduceus (Health), an artisan lathe (Training), migrating footsteps (Migration), and a telegraph (Information).  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:  
"NCERT identifies five sources of human capital formation: schooling, health—including preventive sanitation, on-the-job training, migration for better wages, and labor market information. Together, they multiply total factor productivity across the entire economy."  
*(Right-Side Text Overlay: Animate blue highlight over "5 Sources: Education, Health, Job-Training, Migration, Market Info")*

### [0:28 - 0:42] Page 3: Capital vs. Capability (The Core UPSC Trap)
**Visual**: Page-turn transition. Left side: two antique balance scales: one weighing an engineer's blueprints against gold coins (Human Capital), the other weighing a medical heart and an open book against sunlight (Human Development).  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:  
"Don't fall into this exam trap: Human Capital is instrumental—treating human health and learning purely as tools to raise GDP. Human Development is intrinsic—treating education and dignity as fundamental constitutional rights, regardless of economic output."  
*(Right-Side Text Overlay: Animate gold highlight over "Trap: Human Capital (Means to GDP) vs Human Development (End in Itself)")*
