- **Subtitle**: Ceilings, Floors & Distortions
- **Point 1**: **Price Ceiling**: Fixed below equilibrium; creates shortages requiring rationing.
- **Point 2**: **Price Floor (MSP)**: Fixed above equilibrium; creates surpluses requiring state procurement.
