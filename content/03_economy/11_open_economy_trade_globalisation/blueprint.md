# Topic 11: Architectural Blueprint & Pedagogical Framing

## 1. Syllabus & Exam Integration
- UPSC Prelims:
  - The distinction between the Current Account (Trade in goods, invisible services, unilateral remittances) and the Capital Account (FDI, FII, external debt, official reserves).
  - Autonomous ("Above the Line") versus Accommodating ("Below the Line") transactions.
  - Exchange rate systems: Flexible, Fixed, and Managed Floating regimes.
  - Depreciation vs. Devaluation, and Appreciation vs. Revaluation.
  - Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) and factors driving exchange rate movements.
  - The Open Economy Multiplier formula and the dampening effect of the Marginal Propensity to Import.
  - Asymmetries in global multilateral trade (WTO rules and agricultural subsidies).
- UPSC Mains (GS Paper III - Indian Economy):
  - Current Account Deficit (CAD) vulnerabilities: High dependency on crude oil imports versus service export buffers and remittances.
  - Capital flow volatility: Hot money portfolio inflows (FII) versus stable Foreign Direct Investment (FDI).
  - The impact of post-1991 trade liberalisation on domestic micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSMEs).
  - Designing policies for Fair Globalisation: Reconciling trade competitiveness with labor protections and social security.

## 2. Structural Breakdown (The 5 Core Learning Pillars)
1. Pillar 1: Balance of Payments Framework — Current vs. Capital accounts, BoT vs. Invisibles, and the BoP accounting identity.
2. Pillar 2: Foreign Exchange Mechanisms — Determining exchange rates, market regimes, and currency valuation shifts.
3. Pillar 3: Open Economy Multiplier — Net exports, import leakages, and diminished multiplier potency.
4. Pillar 4: Corporate Globalisation & MNC Strategies — Production integration, foreign investment channels, and 1991 trade reforms.
5. Pillar 5: Distributional Impacts & Fair Globalisation — Consumer gains versus informal labor costs, WTO challenges, and state protection.

## 3. High-Yield Data & Benchmarks
- BoP Identity: Current Account + Capital Account = 0
- Current Account: Balance of Trade + Net Invisibles (Services + Transfers + Factor Income)
- Open Economy Multiplier: Multiplier = 1 / (MPS + Marginal Propensity to Import)
- Managed Floating: Market-driven with intermittent central bank intervention (RBI's operational framework)
- Above the Line: Autonomous transactions driven by commercial profit
- Below the Line: Accommodating transactions designed to restore BoP balance
