**Audio (Voiceover)**:

The Capital Account records international assets like Foreign Direct Investment and overseas loans. Together, they balance out to zero!"

### [0:14 - 0:28] Page 2: Exchange Rates & The RBI Shield
**Visual**: Seamless transition. Left 60%: Isometric cutaway of a central bank foreign exchange vault with dual scales balancing rupee and dollar flows under a regulatory valve. Right 40%: Formatted typography card.  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:  
"Who sets the exchange rate? Under a floating system, market forces decide. Under a fixed peg, the government fixes the price. India follows a Managed Float: letting market supply and demand guide the rupee, while the Reserve Bank of India steps in to smooth out severe shocks."

### [0:28 - 0:42] Page 3: The Open Economy Multiplier
**Visual**: Seamless transition. Left 60%: Cross-section showing domestic factory assembly lines where a portion of consumer spending leaks out through an import drainage pipe. Right 40%: Formatted typography card.  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:  
"Exam trap alert: is the multiplier bigger in an open economy? No, it is smaller! When domestic incomes rise, part of that extra cash is spent on foreign imports. This import leakage diverts demand abroad, dampening the domestic multiplier effect!"
