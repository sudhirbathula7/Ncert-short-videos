# Topic 09: Architectural Blueprint & Pedagogical Framing

## 1. Syllabus & Exam Integration
- UPSC Prelims:
  - Ex ante planned variables versus ex post realized accounting variables.
  - The algebraic relationships connecting MPC, MPS, APC, and APS.
  - The Investment Multiplier formula: k = 1 / (1 - MPC) = 1 / MPS.
  - The operational self-correcting role of unintended inventory accumulation and depletion.
  - The conceptual mechanics behind the Paradox of Thrift.
  - Clear distinctions between Deficient Demand (Deflationary Gap) and Excess Demand (Inflationary Gap).
- UPSC Mains (GS Paper III - Indian Economy):
  - Keynesian demand management: Countercyclical fiscal stimulus during economic downturns via public capital expenditure.
  - The consumption engine: Evaluating how lower-income transfers stimulate the economy due to their high Marginal Propensity to Consume.
  - Structural unemployment versus deficient demand in developing economies like India.

## 2. Structural Breakdown (The 5 Core Learning Pillars)
1. Pillar 1: Foundational Assumptions & Ex Ante Architecture — Fixed prices, ex ante plans, and the linear consumption function.
2. Pillar 2: Equilibrium Output & Effective Demand — The 45-degree line, AD = Y balance, and unintended inventory adjustments.
3. Pillar 3: The Investment Multiplier — Chain reactions of round-by-round spending and its mathematical inverse to MPS.
4. Pillar 4: The Paradox of Thrift — Individual micro-prudence versus macroeconomic aggregate demand collapse.
5. Pillar 5: Output Gaps & Macro Stabilization — Inflationary and deflationary gaps at full employment thresholds.

## 3. High-Yield Conversion Rules & Formulas
- MPC + MPS = 1
- APC + APS = 1
- Aggregate Demand: AD = Autonomous Spending + (MPC * Income)
- Equilibrium Income: Y = Autonomous Spending / MPS
- Investment Multiplier: k = 1 / MPS = 1 / (1 - MPC)
- Deflationary Gap: Shortfall of AD below full-employment output.
- Inflationary Gap: Excess of AD above full-employment output.
