# Topic 16: Architectural Blueprint & Pedagogical Framing

## 1. Syllabus & Exam Integration
- UPSC Prelims:
  - Conceptual boundaries: Social Exclusion and Vulnerability criteria.
  - Calorie requirements: 2,400 rural vs. 2,100 urban calories per person per day.
  - Official monetary benchmarks (Tendulkar Methodology 2011-12): 816 rupees rural vs. 1,000 rupees urban per capita per month.
  - NSSO institutional role and the World Bank 1.90 dollar PPP benchmark.
  - State-specific poverty reduction drivers: Kerala (human capital), West Bengal (land reform), Tamil Nadu/Andhra Pradesh (PDS), Punjab/Haryana (farm productivity).
  - Social group vulnerability rankings: Scheduled Tribes (43 percent) vs. Scheduled Castes (29 percent) vs. National Average (22 percent).
  - Core targeted initiatives: MGNREGA 2005 features (100 days, one-third women reservation), SGSY, PMGY, PMRY.
- UPSC Mains (GS Paper II - Social Justice & GS Paper III - Indian Economy):
  - Multidimensional poverty vs. income poverty: Evaluating the shift from basic biological subsistence to human capability expansion.
  - Feminization of poverty and intra-household distribution skews.
  - The growth-poverty nexus: Why rapid GDP growth failed to rapidly eliminate absolute deprivation without structural labor shifts.
  - Policy evaluation of targeted anti-poverty schemes: Administrative leakages, targeting errors, and scheme consolidation.

## 2. Structural Breakdown (The 5 Core Learning Pillars)
1. Pillar 1: Multidimensional Poverty Architecture — Gandhi's vision, social exclusion mechanisms, and vulnerability profiling.
2. Pillar 2: Measuring the Poverty Line — Calorie benchmarks, Tendulkar expenditure lines, NSSO role, and headcount ratio trajectories.
3. Pillar 3: Disparity Geography & Global Trends — Vulnerable social castes, intra-family skews, state policy divergence, and East Asian vs. African paths.
4. Pillar 4: Structural Roots of Deprivation — Colonial legacy, population drag, uneven Green Revolution, and informal rural debt traps.
5. Pillar 5: Anti-Poverty Interventions & Human Poverty — Two-pillar strategy, MGNREGA entitlements, self-employment schemes, and capability poverty.

## 3. High-Yield Data & Benchmarks
- Official 2011-12 Poverty Headcount: 22 percent (270 million people)
- Rural Calorie vs. Spending: 2,400 calories | 816 rupees per month
- Urban Calorie vs. Spending: 2,100 calories | 1,000 rupees per month
- Global Extreme Poverty Metric: 1.90 dollars per person per day (2011 PPP)
- MGNREGA Statutory Guarantee: 100 days of manual wage labor with 33 percent women reservation
- Most Vulnerable Social Category: Scheduled Tribes at 43 percent below poverty line
