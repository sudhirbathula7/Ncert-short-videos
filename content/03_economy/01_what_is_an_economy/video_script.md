# Topic 01: What Is an Economy? — Video Script (60s)

**Format**: 9:16 Vertical Short / Reel  
**Voice Pace**: Measured, academic delivery (~140 words)  
**Total Duration**: 58 Seconds  

---

### [0:00 - 0:14] Page 1: Scarcity & Opportunity Cost
**Visual**: Clean off-white canvas. Left 60%: Architectural cutaway of a classical municipal balance scale weighing sacks of grain against factory gears. Right 40%: Structured editorial typography card.  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:  
"Every society faces one unavoidable truth: human desires are unlimited, but economic resources are strictly finite. That is scarcity. When you choose to produce more food, you give up industrial machinery. The value of that next-best alternative you sacrificed is your opportunity cost. It governs every economic choice."  

---

### [0:14 - 0:28] Page 2: The Central Triad & The PPF
**Visual**: Seamless transition. Left 60%: Isometric cutaway of an urban planning workshop with an engraved 2D Production Possibility Frontier chart. Right 40%: Formatted typography card.  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:  
"Every economy must answer three questions: What to produce, How to produce, and For whom to produce. Economists map this on the Production Possibility Frontier. Operating on the curve means full efficiency. Fall inside, and resources are wasted. Pushing outside requires new technology or capital."  

---

### [0:28 - 0:42] Page 3: Command vs. Market vs. Mixed
**Visual**: Seamless transition. Left 60%: Isometric cutaway split between a central government planning office and an open merchant trading floor. Right 40%: Formatted typography card.  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:  
"How do nations organize this? In a command economy, the state dictates every production target. In a market economy, the invisible hand of price signals guides producers. But in reality, most nations—including India—run mixed economies, combining market efficiency with social welfare."  

---

### [0:42 - 0:58] Page 4: The Four Factors & Palampur's Warning
**Visual**: Seamless transition. Left 60%: Architectural cutaway of a farmstead showing arable plots, workers, tractors, and an open ledger. Right 40%: Formatted typography card.  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:
