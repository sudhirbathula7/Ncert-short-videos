# Topic 01: Architectural Blueprint & Pedagogical Framing

## 1. Syllabus & Exam Integration
- UPSC Prelims:
  - Micro vs. Macro boundaries; Positive vs. Normative statements.
  - Characteristics of the Production Possibility Frontier (PPF): shifts vs. movements along the curve.
  - Fixed vs. Working Capital classification across rural and industrial contexts.
  - The factor market realities of land fixity, HYV seed yields, and credit market distortions.
- UPSC Mains (GS Paper III - Indian Economy):
  - Allocation vs. Distribution: Market price failures in delivering equitable basic human needs.
  - Evolution of India's mixed economic structure from central planning to post-1991 market orientation.
  - Agrarian resource constraints: Soil exhaustion, falling water tables, and capital access asymmetry.

## 2. Structural Breakdown (The 5 Core Learning Pillars)
1. Pillar 1: Scarcity & Opportunity Cost — The structural origin of economic choice.
2. Pillar 2: The Central Triad & The PPF — What, How, For Whom; graphical frontier of efficiency.
3. Pillar 3: Economic Systems — Command, Market, and Mixed institutional mechanisms.
4. Pillar 4: Scope & Epistemology — Micro vs. Macro and Positive vs. Normative divides.
5. Pillar 5: Factors of Production — Land fixity, labour wage gaps, and fixed vs. working capital.

## 3. High-Yield Data & Definitions
- Opportunity Cost: Value of the next-best forgone alternative.
- Marginal Rate of Transformation (MRT): Ratio of the good sacrificed to the good gained.
- Fixed Capital: Assets reusable across seasons (tractors, tubewells).
- Working Capital: Assets consumed within one cycle (seeds, fertilizer, cash).
