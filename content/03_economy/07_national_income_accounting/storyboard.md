#### Right 40% Typography Content
- **Header**: THE THREE METHODS
- **Subtitle**: Value, Income & Expenditure
- **Point 1**: **Value-Added**: Gross output minus intermediate consumption.
- **Point 2**: **Income Method**: Sum of wages, rent, interest, and corporate profits.
- **Point 3**: **Expenditure Method**: C + I + G + (X - M) measures final demand.
- **Takeaway Card**: *Value Added = Factor Incomes = Final Spending.*
