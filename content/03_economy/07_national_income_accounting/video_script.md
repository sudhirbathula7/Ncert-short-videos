### [0:14 - 0:28] Page 2: The Three Equivalent Methods
**Visual**: Seamless transition. Left 60%: Isometric cutaway of a circular economic flow wheel connecting factory gates, bank ledgers, and retail stores. Right 40%: Formatted typography card.  
**Audio (Voiceover)**:  
"National income can be calculated three ways: by totaling the value added at every production stage, by adding up all factor earnings like wages, rent, interest, and profits, or by summing final expenditures: C plus I plus G plus net exports. Handled correctly, all three yield the exact same total."
