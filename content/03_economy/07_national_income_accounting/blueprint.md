# Topic 07: Architectural Blueprint & Pedagogical Framing

## 1. Syllabus & Exam Integration
- UPSC Prelims:
  - The distinction between final versus intermediate goods based on functional economic use.
  - Stock versus Flow distinctions (Capital stock and inventories vs. investment and national income).
  - The mathematical bridge connecting GDP to National Income (NNP_FC) via Depreciation, NIT, and NFIA.
  - Derivation of Personal Disposable Income from National Income (treatment of corporate taxes and transfer payments).
  - Real vs. Nominal GDP and the comprehensive coverage of the GDP Deflator compared to CPI and WPI.
- UPSC Mains (GS Paper III - Indian Economy):
  - Evaluating GDP as a metric of human well-being: Examining distribution disparities, non-market care work, and green national accounting.
  - The shift to Gross Value Added (GVA) at basic prices under the SNA 2008 framework and its implications for fiscal analysis.
  - Structural analysis of India's aggregate demand components: C, I, G, and X - M.

## 2. Structural Breakdown (The 5 Core Learning Pillars)
1. Pillar 1: Economic Aggregation Foundations — Final vs. intermediate boundaries, capital classifications, and stock vs. flow variables.
2. Pillar 2: The Circular Flow Triad — The equivalence of Product (Value Added), Income (Factor Shares), and Expenditure approaches.
3. Pillar 3: The Macro Accounting Chain — Navigating Gross to Net, Market Price to Factor Cost, and Domestic to National aggregates.
4. Pillar 4: Household Income Realities — Calculating Personal Income and Personal Disposable Income.
5. Pillar 5: Price Adjustments & Welfare Boundaries — Real vs. Nominal GDP, the GDP Deflator, and non-monetary/environmental welfare distortions.

## 3. High-Yield Conversion Rules
- Gross to Net: Subtract Depreciation.
- Domestic to National: Add Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA).
- Market Price to Factor Cost: Subtract Net Indirect Taxes (Indirect Taxes minus Subsidies).
- National Income (NI): NNP_FC.
- Expenditure Equation: GDP_MP = C + I + G + (X - M).
