# Topic 03: Architectural Blueprint & Pedagogical Framing

## 1. Syllabus & Exam Integration
- **UPSC Prelims**:
  - Axiomatic properties of Indifference Curves (convexity, non-intersection, negative slope).
  - Consumer equilibrium mathematical conditions: Tangency of budget line and IC ($MRS = \frac{P_1}{P_2}$).
  - Movement along the demand curve (contraction/expansion) vs. Shift of the demand curve (increase/decrease).
  - Price Elasticity of Demand ($E_d$) across linear demand curves; determinants of elasticity.
  - Total Outlay / Expenditure Method implications for taxation and pricing policies.
- **UPSC Mains (GS Paper III - Indian Economy)**:
  - Policy pricing and consumer welfare: Welfare losses under price ceilings/floors and indirect tax incidence.
  - Targeting public subsidies: Inferior goods (PDS coarse grains) vs. normal goods and self-targeting mechanisms.
  - Elasticity analysis in agricultural pricing: Inelastic farm produce demand triggering farm revenue collapses during bumper harvests.

## 2. Structural Breakdown (The 5 Core Learning Pillars)
1. **Pillar 1: Utility Theory Dualism** — Cardinal (utils, Diminishing MU) vs. Ordinal (monotonicity, ranking).
2. **Pillar 2: Indifference Curve Geometry** — Convexity, MRS, and the logic of non-intersection.
3. **Pillar 3: The Budget Frontier & Tangency Equilibrium** — Objective market constraints vs. subjective preference maps.
4. **Pillar 4: Derivation of Demand & Behavioral Classifications** — Normal vs. Inferior; Substitutes vs. Complements.
5. **Pillar 5: Elasticity Mechanics & Total Expenditure Dynamics** — Linear demand point elasticity and revenue implications.

## 3. High-Yield Data & Formulas
- **Marginal Utility**: $MU = \frac{\Delta TU}{\Delta Q}$.
- **Marginal Rate of Substitution**: $MRS_{12} = \frac{MU_1}{MU_2} = -\frac{\Delta x_2}{\Delta x_1}$.
- **Consumer Equilibrium**: $MRS_{12} = \frac{P_1}{P_2}$.
- **Price Elasticity of Demand**: $E_d = \frac{\% \Delta Q}{\% \Delta P} = \frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta P} \cdot \frac{P}{Q}$.
- **Linear Demand Midpoint**: Elasticity is exactly unitary ($|E_d| = 1$).
