## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. People as a Resource
- Human Resource Definition: Healthy, educated, and motivated individuals who develop natural assets according to societal needs. Human resource is considered the ultimate resource.
- Skill Development Initiatives: Programs like the Pradhan Mantri Kaushal Vikas Yojna aimed at training youth and encouraging employable skills.

### 2. Population Distribution and Density
- Uneven Dispersion: Over ninety percent of the world population lives on roughly thirty percent of land surface. Three-quarters inhabit Asia and Africa.
- Population Density: Calculated as persons per square kilometer, with a global average of fifty-one persons per square kilometer.
- Determinants: Influenced by geographical factors (topography, climate, soil, water, minerals) and socio-economic factors (housing, education, employment, cultural significance).

### 3. Population Change and Composition
- Growth Drivers: Driven by birth rates, death rates, and migration flows (emigrants leaving and immigrants arriving).
- Demographic Explosion: Rapid population doubling times driven by improved healthcare and reduced mortality while birth rates remained high.
- Population Pyramids: Graphical age-sex structures displaying young dependents under fifteen, the working-age cohort, and elderly dependents over sixty-five.
