## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Minerals and Ores
- Mineral definition: A naturally occurring substance with a definite chemical composition. Minerals concentrate in particular rock formations and are identified by physical and chemical properties.
- Rock and Ore: A rock is an aggregate of one or more minerals without a definite composition, while an ore is a rock from which minerals are commercially mined. Out of 2,800+ identified mineral types, only about 100 form commercial ores.
- Classification: 
  - Metallic ferrous (contains iron, such as iron ore and manganese).
  - Metallic non-ferrous (contains other metals like gold, silver, copper, and bauxite).
  - Non-metallic (no metal content, such as limestone, mica, and gypsum).
- Extraction Methods: Open-cast mining for shallow layers, shaft mining for deep deposits, drilling for petroleum and natural gas, and quarrying for surface minerals.
- Global Distribution: Metallic minerals mostly concentrate in igneous and metamorphic rock plateaus, while non-metallic minerals and fossil fuels occur in sedimentary strata across global shields and basins.

### 2. Power and Energy Resources
- Conventional Sources: Includes firewood and fossil fuels (coal, petroleum, natural gas) which are non-renewable.
  - Coal: Known as buried sunshine; abundant fossil fuel used for thermal electricity. Key Indian fields include Jharia, Raniganj, and Bokaro.
  - Petroleum: Known as black gold; dark liquid drilled from coastal and offshore fields, refined into fuel and plastics. Key Indian fields include Digboi and Bombay High.
  - Natural Gas: Found alongside petroleum; clean fuel including CNG.
  - Hydel Power: Generated from falling dam water; supplies roughly one-quarter of world electricity, pioneered by Norway.
- Non-Conventional Sources: Renewable alternatives including solar energy, wind energy, nuclear power (uranium in Rajasthan/Jharkhand, thorium in Kerala sands), geothermal energy (Manikaran, Puga Valley), tidal energy (Gulf of Kachchh), and biogas.
- Conservation: Minimizing mining waste, recycling metals, and adhering to the principle that energy saved is energy generated.
