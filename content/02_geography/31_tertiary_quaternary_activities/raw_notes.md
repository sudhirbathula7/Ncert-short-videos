## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Tertiary Economic Activities Overview
- Service Sector: Focuses on skilled services, professional expertise, and exchange rather than raw material processing.
- Trade and Commerce: Spans rural marketing centers, periodic markets, urban retail structures, and wholesale distribution networks.
- Transport and Communication: Involves physical mobility networks defined by nodes, links, and isochrones, alongside modern telecommunications and internet connectivity.

### 2. Services, Tourism, and Medical Hubs
- Service Classification: Ranges from low-order routine services to high-order professional, personal, and regulated state services.
- Tourism: The world's largest tertiary activity, driven by climate, landscape, history, culture, and economic affordability.
- Medical Tourism: Combining specialized healthcare treatment with international travel, supported by outsourced medical tests and interpretations.

### 3. Quaternary, Quinary Activities, and Outsourcing
- Quaternary Sector: Knowledge-oriented information collection, research, and development dominating advanced economies.
- Quinary Sector: High-level policy and decision-making roles held by gold-collar specialists.
- Outsourcing and KPO: Contracting business processes (BPO) and knowledge processes (KPO) overseas to leverage comparative advantages.
- Digital Divide: Global and internal disparities in access to information and communication technology.
