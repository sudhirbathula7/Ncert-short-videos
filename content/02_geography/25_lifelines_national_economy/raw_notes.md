## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Means of Transportation: Land and Pipelines
- Roadways: Preferred for short distances due to flexibility and lower construction costs. Classified into Golden Quadrilateral, North-South and East-West corridors, National Highways, State Highways, district roads, rural roads, and border roads.
- Railways: The largest rail network in Asia, efficiently moving heavy goods and passengers across long distances. Distribution influenced by physiography, economic activity, and historical factors.
- Pipelines: Used for transporting crude oil, petroleum products, and natural gas over long distances without trans-shipment losses or delays.

### 2. Waterways and Airways
- Inland Waterways: Navigable rivers and canals providing a cheap, fuel-efficient transport mode for bulky goods.
- Major Seaports: Twelve major ports and roughly two hundred minor ports handling about ninety-five percent of foreign trade volume across both the west and east coasts.
- Airways: The fastest and most expensive transport mode, essential for remote, inaccessible regions such as mountainous terrains and islands.

### 3. Communication Networks and International Trade
- Communication Systems: Spans personal postal networks, advanced telecommunication infrastructure, and mass media channels including All India Radio and Doordarshan.
- International Trade: Exchange of goods across national borders, measured by the balance of trade where exports exceed imports in a favorable trade balance.
- Tourism as Trade: Functions as a vital service trade, generating employment for over fifteen million people and promoting cultural integration.
