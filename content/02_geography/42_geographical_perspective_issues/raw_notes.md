## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Changing Trade Profile and Deficits
- Volume Expansion: External trade has grown exponentially, though import values consistently outpace export earnings, creating a structural trade deficit.
- Historical Policy Shifts: Post-independence import substitution gave way to export promotion, trade liberalization, and tariff reductions.

### 2. Export and Import Composition Shifts
- Exports: Dominated by manufactured goods (engineering items, gems, and jewellery), alongside rising shares of refined petroleum products and fluctuating agricultural shares.
- Imports: The Green Revolution eliminated foodgrain import needs, shifting focus toward petroleum, coal, fertilizers, and specialized capital goods.

### 3. Direction of Trade and Maritime Gateways
- Trading Partners: Broad multilateral trade spanning Asia, Europe, North America, Latin America, and Africa, with minor land-borne trade with immediate neighbors.
- Major Ports: Divided between the west coast (Deendayal, Mumbai, JNPT, Mormugao, New Mangalore, Cochin) and east coast (Kolkata, Haldia, Paradwip, Visakhapatnam, Chennai, Kamarajar, Tuticorin), serving expansive inland hinterlands.
