## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Fundamentals and History of Trade
- Trade Definition: Voluntary exchange of goods and services between parties, evolving from ancient barter systems to currency and global commerce.
- Historical Evolution: Spans the ancient Silk Route, European maritime expansion, colonial trade, and the Industrial Revolution's demand for raw materials.
- Basis of Trade: Rooted in uneven resource distribution, population characteristics, stages of economic development, and foreign capital investment.

### 2. Trade Mechanics and Institutional Frameworks
- Balance of Trade: Measures the value of exports versus imports, resulting in favorable surpluses or unfavorable deficits.
- Trade Agreements: Operates via bilateral or multilateral arrangements and free trade liberalisation.
- WTO and Blocs: The World Trade Organisation governs global trade rules, complemented by regional trade blocs generating over half of global trade.

### 3. Ports as Trade Gateways
- Port Classification by Cargo: Industrial, commercial, and comprehensive ports.
- Port Classification by Location: Inland ports and deep-water out ports.
- Specialized Ports: Oil ports, ports of call, packet stations, entrepôt collection hubs, and strategic naval ports.
