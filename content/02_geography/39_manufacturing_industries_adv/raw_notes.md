## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Planning Approaches and Institutions
- Sectoral vs Regional Planning: Sectoral planning focuses on economic sectors like agriculture and manufacturing, while regional planning adopts a spatial perspective to reduce regional imbalances.
- Institutional Evolution: Centrally managed planning by the Planning Commission transitioned to decentralized multi-level planning and the establishment of NITI Aayog on January 1, 2015.
- Target Area Planning: Introduced to arrest worsening regional disparities through specific area and target group development programmes.

### 2. Hill and Tribal Case Studies
- Drought Prone Area Programme (DPAP): Initiated in the 4th Five Year Plan, shifting from civil works to integrated watershed development.
- Bharmaur ITDP Case Study: Integrated Tribal Development Project in Bharmaur, Himachal Pradesh, targeting the Gaddi tribal community, yielding major social gains like female literacy rising to 65 percent.

### 3. Sustainable Development and Canal Irrigation
- Brundtland Definition: Sustainable development defined in the 1987 report "Our Common Future" as meeting present needs without compromising future generations.
- Indira Gandhi Canal Command Area: Transformed the Thar Desert ecology through Stage-I and Stage-II irrigation, while introducing severe ecological challenges like waterlogging and soil salinity.
- Restorative Measures: Seven proposed sustainability strategies including strict water management, cropping pattern control, and the warabandi water distribution system.
