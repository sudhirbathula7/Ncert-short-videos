## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Land-Use Categories and Changes
- Reporting Area: Maintained by land revenue records across nine standard categories, tracking forests, wastelands, non-agricultural uses, fallows, and net sown area.
- Structural Shifts: Expanding economies drive increased non-agricultural land use, while agricultural land experiences continuous pressure despite declining GDP shares.
- Common Property Resources (CPRs): Community-owned natural resources providing fodder and fuel, vital for landless farmers and women.

### 2. Cropping Systems and Farming Typologies
- Cropping Seasons: Spans Kharif (southwest monsoon), Rabi (winter), and Zaid (summer) seasons.
- Farming Types: Classified by moisture sources into irrigated (protective versus productive) and rainfed systems (dryland versus wetland farming).
- Major Crops: Comprehensive distribution of cereals (rice, wheat, jowar, bajra, maize), pulses (gram, tur), oilseeds (groundnut, mustard, soyabean), fibres (cotton, jute), and cash/plantation crops (sugarcane, tea, coffee).

### 3. Agricultural Development and Structural Problems
- Green Revolution: Adopted package technology (HYVs, fertilizers, irrigation) in the mid-1960s, achieving foodgrain self-sufficiency.
- Sustainability Initiatives: National Mission for Sustainable Agriculture, Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana, and the Farmer's Portal.
- Systemic Challenges: Monsoon dependence, low productivity, indebtedness, inadequate land reforms, small holdings, underemployment, and land degradation (waterlogging, salinization).
