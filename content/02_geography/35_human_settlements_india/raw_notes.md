## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Fundamentals of Settlements and Rural Typologies
- Settlement Definition: Clusters of dwellings where humans live, grouping people and apportioning territory as a resource base.
- Rural versus Urban: Rural settlements rely on land-based primary activities, whereas urban settlements focus on manufacturing, processing, and services.
- Rural Settlement Types: Governed by physical, cultural, and security factors into four broad types: clustered/agglomerated, semi-clustered, hamleted, and dispersed.

### 2. Urban Definition and Historical Evolution
- Census Urban Definition: Requires a municipal body, population of at least 5,000, density of 400 persons per square kilometer, and 75 percent male non-agricultural workers.
- Ancient and Medieval Towns: Ancient religious centers like Varanasi and Prayagraj; medieval fort and capital towns like Delhi, Hyderabad, and Jaipur.
- Modern Towns: Developed by Europeans as trading ports (Mumbai, Kolkata, Surat), industrial hubs (Jamshedpur), and post-independence planned capitals (Chandigarh, Gandhinagar).

### 3. Urbanization Trends and Functional Classification
- Urbanization Level: Reached 31.16 percent in 2011, reflecting rapid total urban growth despite a slower growth rate in recent decades.
- Functional Categories: Towns are classified by primary functions into administrative, industrial, transport, commercial, mining, garrison, educational, religious, and tourist centers.
- Smart Cities Mission: Government initiative to promote sustainable, tech-enabled urban infrastructure and inclusive development.
