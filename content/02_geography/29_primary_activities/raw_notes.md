## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Primary Economic Activities Overview
- Sector Definition: Economic activities generating income, divided into primary, secondary, tertiary, and quaternary sectors. Primary activities directly depend on environmental resources.
- Red-Collar Workers: Individuals engaged in outdoor primary activities like hunting, gathering, agriculture, and mining.

### 2. Gathering, Pastoralism, and Agriculture
- Gathering and Pastoralism: Oldest economic activities practiced in harsh latitudes or tropical forests. Nomadic herding relies on animal migration, while commercial ranching uses fenced pastures.
- Agricultural Typologies: Ranges from primitive subsistence shifting cultivation and intensive wet-paddy farming to large-scale colonial plantation agriculture and extensive mechanized grain farming.
- Specialized Farming: Includes mixed farming, dairy farming, Mediterranean viticulture, truck farming, factory farming, co-operative farming, and Soviet collective farming (Kolkhoz).

### 3. Mining and Extraction Economics
- Mining Profitability: Governed by physical factors (deposit size, grade, occurrence) and economic factors (technology, infrastructure, demand, transport).
- Extraction Methods: Divided into surface open-cast mining for shallow deposits and underground shaft mining for deep-lying ores.
- Global Production Trends: Developed economies retreat from mining due to labor costs, while developing nations become major mineral producers.
