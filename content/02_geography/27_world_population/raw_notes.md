## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Distribution and Density
- Real Wealth: People constitute a country's real wealth by shaping policies and utilizing resources.
- Distribution Patterns: Extremely uneven; roughly ninety percent of the world population lives on about ten percent of the land area.
- Population Density: Calculated as population divided by area, expressed in persons per square kilometer.
- Influencing Factors: Governed by geographical factors (water, landforms, climate, soils), economic factors (minerals, urbanization, industrialization), and sociocultural conditions.

### 2. Growth and Components of Population Change
- Growth Metrics: Evaluated via natural growth (births minus deaths) and actual growth (births minus deaths plus net migration).
- Crude Rates: Crude Birth Rate and Crude Death Rate measured per one thousand population.
- Migration: Spontaneous movement from a place of origin to a place of destination driven by push and pull factors.

### 3. Demographic Transition and Population Control
- Demographic Transition Theory: Explains population changes as societies transition from rural agrarian conditions to urban industrial settings across three distinct stages.
- Population Control: Implemented via family planning measures, awareness campaigns, and contraceptive access.
- Malthusian Theory: Thomas Malthus argued that population grows faster than food supply, requiring preventive checks to avoid natural disasters like famine and war.
