## Core Factive & Conceptual Architecture

### 1. Road and Rail Transport Networks
- Road Dominance: Roads carry roughly eighty-five percent of passenger traffic and seventy percent of freight traffic. Major initiatives include the Golden Quadrilateral, North-South/East-West corridors, and Bharatmala Pariyojana.
- Strategic Infrastructure: Border Roads Organisation develops remote and high-altitude networks, exemplified by the 9.02-kilometer Atal Tunnel.
- Railways: Indian Railways is the largest government undertaking, spanning 67,956 kilometers across broad, metre, and narrow gauges, highlighted by the Konkan Railway engineering marvel.

### 2. Waterways, Airways, and Pipelines
- Inland Waterways: National Waterways Act 2016 designated 111 waterways, including NW1 (Prayagraj-Haldia) and NW2 (Brahmaputra).
- Oceanic Trade: Over ninety-five percent of India's foreign trade volume moves through twelve major and two hundred minor ports.
- Civil Aviation: Expanding rapidly supported by the UDAN Regional Connectivity Scheme and Pawan Hans helicopter services.
- Pipelines: Efficient liquid and gas transit managed by OIL and GAIL, featuring cross-country lines like the Hazira-Vijaipur-Jagdishpur (HVJ) pipeline.

### 3. Communication and Space Technology
- Communication Media: Comprises personal internet networks and mass media broadcasters like All India Radio (Akashvani) and Doordarshan.
- Satellite Systems: INSAT supports multipurpose telecommunication and meteorology, while IRS satellites provide remote sensing data processed by the National Remote Sensing Centre in Hyderabad.
